## Register for the CLaS Shared Task

We invite researchers and teams to participate in the CLaS Shared Task at SemEval 2027.

Please complete the registration form below to indicate your team's interest and the tracks you plan to participate in. You can update your plans as your work develops.

Registration is intended to help the organizers communicate with participating teams. **Registering does not commit your team to a particular method or model.**

Registration information will be used to coordinate the shared task and communicate relevant updates.
