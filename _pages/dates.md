### System evaluation

During the evaluation period (January 10–31, 2027), each team will be allowed **two test submissions per track and one final submission per track**. The final submission will be used for the official rankings.

The organizers will run submitted systems on their own hardware. Detailed submission instructions, including code and dependency requirements, will be announced closer to the evaluation period.

Registration will be handled through this website. Further information will be published as it becomes available.
